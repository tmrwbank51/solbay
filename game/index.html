HOW TO PLAY

Explore the city on foot or by car. Press E near any car to take it — stealing raises your wanted level. The police chase you at 1 star and up: break line of sight and stay far away to cool off. If the bust meter fills while you crawl, you are busted; if your health hits zero, you are wasted. Missions pay cash — open them with M or walk to a green beacon. Press B near an orange marker to buy a taco (full health) or near a blue marker to repair your car at Vic's Garage, and pick up the fare at the yellow taxi stand to earn tips. Your game saves when you finish a mission, and from the pause menu.
